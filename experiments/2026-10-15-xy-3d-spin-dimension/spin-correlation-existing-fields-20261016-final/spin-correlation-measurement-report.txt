LONG-DISTANCE SPIN CORRELATIONS: 3D beta=0, EXISTING FIELDS
Measured October16,2026. Exploratory finite-size cross-check.

Outcome
The retained fields support direct G_L(L/4) and G_L(L/2) measurements on L=24,32,48,64 at eight measured epsilon/pi coordinates. The separated-correlator quotients are useful cross-checks but do not stabilize the asymptotic scaling dimension or provide a calibrated systematic uncertainty. No new MC trajectories were generated.

Observable and samples
G_L(r) = (3V)^(-1) sum_{a=1}^3 sum_x cos(phi(x+r e_a)-phi(x)).
Each whole configuration is one MC observation; spatial sites and directions are averaged, never counted as independent samples. Complex per-axis correlations are retained in the derived histories.
64 existing chains = 32 coordinates times two independent starts. epsilon/pi = .8005,.800625,.80075,.800875,.801,.801125,.80125,.8015 (actual source coordinates preserved).
Chains have12000 or20000 cycles. Every100-cycle checkpoint was measured, including warmup in the saved histories. The primary cut discards cycles<=1000:110 or190 fields/start,9600 fields after cut in total;10304 fields including warmup. Adaptive/dense retained-field windows were not pooled into G.

Statistical method
All recorded scalar m2,S0,F histories are used. A field at cycle100k shares its time bin with scalar observations from100k-99 through100k. The same contiguous-bin block indices jointly resample all observables, independently within each start; pooled means use observation counts. R=xi/L is formed from resampled mean S0 and mean F. Logs are applied to ensemble G means, never individual signed configurations.
Block widths use checkpoint G/m2/R/winding and dense-scalar autocorrelation estimates in physical cycle units, with late-half checks. Primary factor2 gives2–17 conservative blocks/start, all below50. These estimates are too sparse to certify mixing or bootstrap coverage. The doubled-width check can leave only one block for a required group.
At each pair L:2L, solve R_L(q)=R_2L(q), interpolate log G at that q, and compute Delta_eff=-log[G_2L(alpha*2L)/G_L(alpha*L)]/(2 log2). R itself is interpolated; this differs from interpolating raw S0/F components.
Every bootstrap draw must have exactly one supported crossing; missing/multiple/invalid roots remain explicit. Linear/PCHIP and prespecified narrow/full windows are reported. A resolved label requires>=99% common-valid draws and is only a support rule, not coverage certification. Quarter/half results share covariance and are not independent measurements.

Checks and limitations
Baseline24:48 linear retains1983/2000 draws; PCHIP retains1878/2000 and is unresolved because of multiple crossings. Baseline32:64 retains2000/2000 for both interpolators. Narrow/full windows share the root neighborhood, so their agreement is not independent validation.
The checkpoint-only scalar comparison has unresolved crossing tails in both pairs. Doubled blocks leave the32:64 pair unresolved because a required L32,q=.8005 group has fewer than two blocks/start. This is insufficient statistical information, not a demonstrated mixing failure.
Primary half-chain flags occur at L24,q=.801 and L32,q=.80125; L32,q=.8005 has insufficient half-block counts. All flags, starts, invalid tails and omitted groups remain in the JSON.
The larger block choice makes the fixed-q fitted SD smaller, not reliably more precise: with few blocks it is an uncertainty-estimation sensitivity. No precision improvement is claimed.
The paired quarter-G minus dense-m2 exponent intervals contain zero for both pairs. An isolated half-G/m2 difference is not robust across sampling checks; it does not establish a physical discrepancy or bias correction.
At fixed q=.80075 the joint leading G slope is .542826+/- .008247 stat (chi2=4.247/5). This fixes q and omits finite-size corrections, so its small statistical error is not an asymptotic CFT error bar.
Only two size pairs are available. No validated infinite-volume extrapolation or numerical sigma_sys is reported. Measuring at nonzero separation avoids the particular integrated-m2 short-distance contact background, but finite-size, thermal and sampling effects remain.
