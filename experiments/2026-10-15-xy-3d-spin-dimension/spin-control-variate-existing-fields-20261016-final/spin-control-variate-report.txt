CROSS-FITTED SPIN-CORRELATION CONTROL VARIATE: EXISTING-DATA TRIAL
October16,2026. No new MC, field extraction, archive modification or environment change.

Outcome
The estimator is promising for reducing statistical noise. On the same time-block bootstrap draws, median raw-G statistical SD ratios are .538 (r=L/4) and .539 (r=L/2), before positivity or crossing filtering. This is an estimated conditional variance gain, not demonstrated independent repeated-run performance or a bias correction.
It does not establish a controlled infinite-volume scaling dimension or numerical systematic uncertainty. The simple fixed-q common-slope model remains sensitive to analysis choices and has poor nominal fits in the sampling variants.

Data and estimator
Same64 measured chains on32 coordinates, L=24,32,48,64 and8 epsilon/pi values at beta=0, with two independent starts per coordinate. The original source measurement has9600 postcut regularly retained configurations. All existing measurements and previous analyses are preserved.
For start a, Gcv_a = G_a - c_b*(m2CP_a - m2dense_a), where c_b is trained on the OTHER independent start b; exchange starts and pool by row counts.
The coefficient is raw-configuration Cov(G,m2CP)/Var(m2CP), separately for r=L/4 and L/2. This is a prespecified heuristic, not necessarily the variance-optimal coefficient for correlated mean differences.
The control difference has zero ensemble mean under stationarity: CP and dense measurements target the same m2 expectation. Independence of starts allows cross-training without using evaluation data to choose its coefficient. Independent initialization alone does not prove stationarity.
Dense scalar mean uncertainty is retained. Augmented100-cycle-bin moments jointly carry sparse G, CP m2, dense m2/S0/F, winding and training products. Resample time blocks independently within each start and refit BOTH cross-coefficients in every draw.
Blocks use the maximum of original monitored widths and new corrected-series full/late-half autocorrelation widths. The same augmented draw bank produces the original and CV estimators. Therefore matched-original SDs differ slightly from the older non-CV delivery; the comparison here uses identical resampling.
Logs are taken only after ensemble averaging. Unlogged raw-G variance comparisons include all finite draws, before positivity or root filtering. Training singularities, negative CV means, missing roots and insufficient blocks are not clipped or silently removed.

Why no new CFT Delta is quoted

The existing data support using the CV as a promising statistical diagnostic; they do not resolve the remaining sampling/finite-size questions.
