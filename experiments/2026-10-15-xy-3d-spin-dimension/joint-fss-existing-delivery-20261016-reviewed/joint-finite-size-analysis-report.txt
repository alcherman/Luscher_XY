3D beta=0: existing-data joint finite-size scaling analysis
16 October 2026

OUTCOME
The new analysis propagates critical-epsilon uncertainty and demonstrates a specific background/correction ambiguity. It does NOT establish a calibrated systematic standard deviation. No new simulations were launched.

Input bank: 48 coordinate groups, 96 independently seeded chains, L=12,16,24,32,48,64, q=.8005 through .8015. Chain lengths: {12000: 64, 20000: 32}; full source-pinned parent cohort has270 trajectories. Narrow window .800625:.801000 uses24 groups/48 chains. Source means/joint bootstrap caches are unchanged.

MODEL AND STATISTICS
Fit R=xi/L and log<m2> jointly. ell=L/32, x=1000(q-qc)*ell^(1/nu), u=ell^-omega.
Baseline R=Rstar+r1*x+r2*x^2+bR*u.
Baseline log<m2>=a-2*Delta*log(ell)+m1*x+m2*x^2+bm*u.
The two responses share qc. Rstar and Delta are free; no critical R or Delta target imposed.
nu=.67169 and omega=.789 are explicitly Wilson-Fisher-informed assumptions. Separate nu=.63/.71, omega=.6/1 stress checks are descriptive, not confidence ranges.
Variant terms: x*u in both observables; analytic thermal amplitude in logm2; u^2; cubic x^3. Raw-m2 variants fit exp(singular log response)+Bphysical/L^3, with signed background and analytic Jacobian.
Profile qc on q=.80025:.80175; globally scan/refine its minima and solve remaining linear GLS coefficients. Full nonlinear raw fits use12 central starting points. Joint rank/conditioning, optimizer success, boundary hits and nonfinite parameters and exponent clipping are checked on each bootstrap draw.
Within-coordinate 2x2 covariance retained, different independently sampled coordinates treated independent. Fixed central whitening and original joint bootstrap draws reused across all model/size fits. No noisy cross-coordinate sample covariance is used for weighting.
Baseline: cut1001, block factor2, 2000 draws. Separate saved latecut4001, block factor4 and even/odd-start banks are fitted as sampling checks. Across-bank differences are descriptive, not paired significance tests.

The regular-background coefficient corresponds to Bphysical=13.5388, i.e. Bphysical/L^3 in <m2>. This term is allowed because <m2> integrates correlations including short separations. Its fitted fraction is about8.5% atL12,1.2% atL32,0.3% atL64 (conditional on this model). Small corrections can mimic a different exponent over a finite size range. Signed analytic backgrounds are not removed by an arbitrary sign/O(1) prior.
The observed size-cut and second-correction shifts remain statistically compatible with zero in these paired tests. They cannot themselves be declared measured biases. The regular-background estimator shift is conditionally resolved in this family, but this does not prove the model is the true asymptotic form or define sigma_sys.

EXPLORATORY FOLLOW-UP
After observing broad-window quadratic prediction misses, the post-hoc cubic variant no longer rejects the nominal joint test64 comparison (p=.134), but its absolute errors do not improve. Max m2 mismatch grows10.21% to11.87%; log-m2 RMS error .03935 to .04467. Atq=.8015 predictive SD grows .04165 to .09140 while central log residual grows .09723 to .11219. Higher p reflects changed/wider uncertainty, not demonstrated predictive improvement or an isolated thermal-truncation cause. The train<=32 cubic even has maxm2 mismatch about207% despite p=.547, showing how weak extrapolations give uninformative prediction tests. The full broad cubic estimate .52016 +/- .00509stat is a conditional post-hoc model result, not a selected final value or independent validation.
Post-hoc raw-background size-cut fits remain near .55 but weaken: minL16 .55354 +/- .03060stat, minL24 .55164 +/- .04879stat. Dropping smaller boxes does not make the existing data constrain the background tightly.

WHAT CAN BE CLAIMED
A narrow-window leading joint fit gives Delta=.5182 +/- .0069stat, compatible with prior quotient/slope illustrations near.52. This is a conditional estimate, not the final CFT dimension with total uncertainty.
Critical-epsilon uncertainty is now propagated rather than fixed, but regular-background/correction freedom remains a practical ambiguity. A small numerical sigma_sys or two reliable CFT digits have not been established. No model is selected by best residual p or forced to the known O(2) benchmark.
Next leverage would come from a larger size or an observable less exposed to short-distance background; this report does not authorize or launch either. Larger predictive p alone does not validate a model or set systematic coverage.
