The completed September 2 scout and first refinement are immutable inputs. The package source and update kernel are unchanged. Each pair has L=16,32,64 and four independent cold/winding starts, each with 32000 hybrid cycles. A cycle is one full Metropolis sweep, four fixed Wolff clusters and one winding proposal. This gives 128000 sweeps and 512000 clusters per parameter/volume group; a provisional 2000-cycle cut leaves 120000 retained cycles, three times the previous refined count. Histories must support the cut before accepting the result.

The new root seed is distinct from previous campaigns. Every initial state, cycle field, observable, RNG record and work/timing measurement is saved. Completed files receive the same independent full-field physical audit. Checkpoint/resume parameters and source hashes are enforced. The initial batch budgets about 36.3 GiB plus the existing 25 GiB reserve; there was about 66 GiB free at design time. Further frozen batches require enough space, or a user-provided storage location; no old data may be deleted to make room.

Adaptive priorities after the initial results: epsilon/pi near 0.555–0.575 at kappa=0, intermediate epsilon slices near 0.585 and 0.595, then 0.60–0.64 if their bars remain too wide. Choose new couplings at 0.01 resolution from actual edge diagnostics. Do not fill an expensive rectangular grid. Statistical effort and further grid refinement can reduce sampling and localization error; the endpoint-model envelope is still a finite-volume heuristic and can impose a nonzero limiting width.
