Talk-safe claims and caveats are in the report. The pale errors are descriptive sensitivity unions, not calibrated total confidence intervals or systematic standard deviations. D5 q=1 is a rejected-fit diagnostic. Delta near .52 is exploratory; no calibrated stat+sys CFT determination is claimed. The 3D phase-curve endpoint table and later finite-pair common-xi crossings use different estimators and must not be conflated.
